How to book drop-in ice & sports

Many drop-in skates and gym times are walk-in, but most stick & puck, drop-in hockey and a lot of gym sports (pickleball especially) need a spot booked ahead. Here's how each city does it, for ice and for gym & court sports. In both finders (ice times and drop-in sports), sessions that need booking have a Book button.

Before you book

  • Make your account first. Each city has its own free account, and setting one up takes a few minutes, so do it before booking opens.
  • Set a reminder for when booking opens. Popular stick & puck, hockey and pickleball times can fill within minutes.
  • Cancel if you can't go. Some cities charge for no-shows.

Surrey Some sessions

Ice

Hockey, stick & puck and figure skating: sign up 72 hours ahead. Public skating: no sign-up, pay at the door.

  • Sign-up opens exactly 72 hours before the session starts.
  • You need a free City of Surrey recreation account to register.
  • Hockey and stick & puck are on the Drop In Hockey list; figure skating is on the Drop In Skating list.
Drop In Hockey (hockey, stick & puck) ↗Drop In Skating (figure skating) ↗

Or call 604-501-5100.

Gym & court sports

Most drop-in sports open for sign-up 72 hours ahead; pickleball opens Sundays at 8:30 pm. If you're not there within 10 minutes of the start, your spot goes to the waitlist.

Richmond Some sessions

Ice

Public skates are drop-in. Stick & puck, hockey and figure skating are pre-registered.

  • Online booking opens at 6:00 am on Monday, one week before the session (phone from 8:30 am).
  • You pay when you book. Drop-ins are welcome if there's space left.
  • On the arenas page, use the “Registered Visits” link for Richmond Ice Centre or Minoru Arenas.

Or call 604-276-4300.

Gym & court sports

Gym and racquet drop-ins are walk-in. A few sessions are registered visits or need a phone call; the finder notes which.

Burnaby Some sessions

Ice

Most skates are drop-in. Sessions marked “Reserve” can be booked ahead.

  • Reserve sessions open 48 hours ahead for Burnaby residents and 24 hours ahead for everyone else.
  • Book on WebReg with a free Burnaby account.

Port Coquitlam Booking needed

Ice

Drop-in skates and hockey are pre-registered.

  • Booking opens 48 hours ahead: 8 am for Port Coquitlam residents, 10 am for everyone else.
  • You need an account. Online booking closes 30 minutes before the start.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours ahead, or a no-show is charged.

North Vancouver (NVRC) Some sessions

Ice

Public skates are drop-in. Drop-in hockey is booked as a “book-a-visit” session.

  • Sports sessions open for booking at 7 am on Tuesday the week before.
  • Book with an NVRC account, and cancel if you can't make it so someone else gets the spot.
